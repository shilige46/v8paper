## Proof accessibility

Good. The multiple-copy Ramsey paper gives an explicit general framework for `mG` and `nH`, where `mG` is a union of `m` vertex-disjoint copies of `G`. Its published summary gives an effective two-sided bound and exact evaluations in important cases, including disjoint triangles.

## Proof structure profile

Finite Ramsey packing. Likely layers:

1. Define a monochromatic copy of a fixed finite graph in a two-coloured complete graph.
2. Define a family of vertex-disjoint copies / support set.
3. Prove packing bookkeeping and residual-vertex lemmas.
4. Apply ordinary finite Ramsey bounds to the remaining graph repeatedly or use the paper's sharper induction.
5. Derive the scoped uncovered-vertex bound in the JSP statement.

## Likely Lean / Mathlib prerequisites

- graph embeddings/isomorphisms/subgraphs
- finite partitions / two-colourings
- disjoint finite-set families

## Formalization bottlenecks

- Mathlib matching is edge matching; the proof needs disjoint *vertex sets of graph copies*. A small project-local packing abstraction is likely cleaner than forcing everything into edge matching.
- The catalog's natural-language scope must be matched to one exact theorem/corollary from the 1975 paper.
- Avoid formalizing the entire general Ramsey-number theory when only a scoped packing result is needed.
