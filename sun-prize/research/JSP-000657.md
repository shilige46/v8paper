## Proof accessibility

Strong. The solution paper is five pages. Its abstract states a concrete theorem: for any `n`-vertex graph in which no two vertices of degree at least 3 are adjacent, the Ramsey number is at most `12n`. This directly settles the relevant Burr–Erdős problem and includes subdivisions as a corollary.

## Proof structure profile

Short finite extremal/Ramsey argument. The paper should be decomposed into: structural control of the graph after separating high-degree vertices; embedding/coloring lemmas; a linear-size host guarantee. The exact constants and auxiliary lemmas need paper-level extraction before Lean coding.

## Formalization bottlenecks

- Introducing a fully general Ramsey-number API would be overengineering; the formalization should target only the quantified host/coloring statement needed for this theorem.
- Care is needed around “vertices of degree at least 3 are pairwise nonadjacent” and graph-copy embeddings.
