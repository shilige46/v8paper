Moderate. The official source chain connects balanced multipartite clique forcing to independent-transversal / list-colouring methods. Haxell's 2001 paper is only three pages and proves a clean list-colouring theorem, but the exact path from that theorem to the JSP statement must be reconstructed rather than assumed.

## Proof structure profile

The likely useful duality is: a transversal clique in a multipartite graph is a transversal independent set in its complement. The proof can therefore be organized around part sets, degree bounds between parts, and a Hall/Haxell-style transversal existence lemma.
