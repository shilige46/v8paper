## Proof accessibility

Good. A public copy of the 1990 paper is available. It develops “descending waves” explicitly as one of several generalizations of arithmetic progressions. The relevant problem is finite and combinatorial: under every two-colouring of an interval, force a monochromatic increasing sequence whose successive gaps are nonincreasing and determine the sharp quadratic threshold.
