# JSP-000897 · Dense neighborhoods at the Turán threshold

> **RETIRED / NO-GO as of 2026-09-18.** Do not spend proof-development time on this target unless the public-priority situation changes materially.

## Why it was initially attractive

The mathematical problem is unusually well aligned with Mathlib: finite simple graphs, Turán numbers, maximum degree, neighborhoods and induced-subgraph edge counts are already available. The 1983 Bondy note is short and the central maximum-degree proof has a clean counting structure.

That made JSP-000897 a good *technical* formalization candidate, but technical tractability is not enough when public formalization work already exists.

## Deep race audit correction

Therefore the catalog's pinned `Lean proof = No` status is stale as a competition signal. It means only that the qualifying proof had not yet been accepted/recorded in that catalog snapshot.

## Mathematical statement

At a high level, if an `n`-vertex graph is at the Turán threshold for excluding a clique, the problem asks whether one can find a high-degree vertex whose neighborhood is itself at the next Turán-density threshold. Bollobás–Thomason solved the problem; Bondy strengthened it so the witness can be chosen as a maximum-degree vertex.

A standard proof route bounds the total edge count by the edges inside a maximum-degree neighborhood plus edges meeting its complement, and compares that with a Turán construction obtained by adjoining an independent part.

## Technical value retained

It should be studied as a template, not attacked as a prize target.

## Screening lesson

Before promoting any future candidate, search all of:

1. official awards PRs;
2. official issues/evidence batches;
3. public repositories;
4. global code search;
5. `plby/lean-proofs` by original Erdős number/title;
6. recent source-paper and verification activity.

Repository-name search alone is no longer accepted as race clearance.
